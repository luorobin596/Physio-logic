# Impression Templates

## Core template (full)

Current findings suggest [primary functional deficit or working hypothesis] at [region/side/segment], associated with [symptom behavior] during [task/position]. [Proposed explanation] remains a working hypothesis supported by [case evidence], with [limitations or alternatives]. Relevant contributing factors include [case-supported factors], with limitations in [activity/participation]. Stage: [value and basis]. Irritability: [value and basis]. Key evidence: [available relevant findings]. Prognosis: [judgment and basis, or Insufficient information to determine at present]. Red flags: [findings and assessment limits, or Insufficient information to determine at present].

## Lean clinic-note template (concise)

[Primary working hypothesis @ region/side]; associated task/symptom: [case relationship]. Contributing: [supported factors]. Stage/Irritability: [value and basis]/[value and basis]. Evidence: [available findings]. Limits: [material uncertainty].

## Mechanism-focused template

[Case-supported functional issue or working hypothesis] with [possible pain contribution and evidence] during [task/position]. Contributing: [supported factors]. Limits activities/participation: [provided information]. Evidence and alternatives: [findings and uncertainty].

## ICF-linked template

Body functions: [supported findings or unknown] at [region]. Activity: [provided limitations]. Participation: [provided impact]. Evidence: [findings and limitations]. Stage/Irritability: [value and basis]/[value and basis].

## Sport-load template

In [documented sport/context], [case-supported issue] at [region/side] is associated with [symptoms] during [documented task/load]. Contributors: [supported factors]. Evidence and alternatives: [findings and uncertainty]. Stage/Irritability: [value and basis]/[value and basis].

## Central / Nociplastic-context template

[Supported functional findings or unknown] with a possible nociplastic contribution [case basis and missing information]. Symptoms: [reported pattern]. Contributing factors: [case-supported factors, open scope]. Activity/participation: [provided impact]. Evidence, alternatives and assessment limits: [details].

## Psychosocial-behavioral context template

[Supported functional findings or unknown] with [reported/observed protective behaviors and relevant context]. Association with symptoms/tasks: [case-supported relationship and uncertainty]. Other contributing factors: [open scope]. Evidence and limits: [details]. Stage/Irritability: [value and basis]/[value and basis].

## ANS-related context template

[Reported/observed findings] raise the question of [possible ANS-related contribution], with [uncertainty and alternatives]. Task/context: [provided information]. Other contributing factors: [open scope]. Functional impact: [provided information]. Evidence, missing data and next assessment question: [details].

## Mixed-context template

[Case-supported primary hypothesis] with possible [additional contribution] during [task/context]. Relevant factors: [open scope, case-supported]. Evidence for each contribution: [findings]. Alternatives/unknowns: [details]. Stage/Irritability: [value and basis]/[value and basis].
