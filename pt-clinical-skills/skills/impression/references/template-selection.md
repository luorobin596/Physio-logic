# Choosing an Impression format

Default: Core template (full). A full record remains the default even for an athletic or complex case; offer a more fitting alternative after completing it, or follow a user's explicit requested version. Explain an alternative's relevance, ask once and wait. Do not produce all variants automatically.

- Lean clinic-note: concise documentation after a full synthesis or explicit request.
- Mechanism-focused: useful when case-supported mechanism hypotheses are the discussion focus.
- ICF-linked: useful when body function, activity and participation relationships need explicit separation.
- Sport-load: useful for documented sport/task/load demands.
- Central/nociplastic, psychosocial-behavioral, ANS-related or mixed-context variants: only when case evidence supports discussing that context; do not use the title to establish the condition or restrict contributing factors to psychology.

Source: Impression Templates.pdf (5 pages), adapted under R01–R06. Candidate context templates do not establish diagnostic criteria. No breathing prescriptions or unverified causal wording is imported from examples. Unknown Stage/Irritability, Prognosis and Red flags remain explicitly unknown where included; do not force a deficit, a mechanism or 2–4 tests.
